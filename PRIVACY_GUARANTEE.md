# 🔒 Nikki 100% Privacy & Data Security Guarantee

Nikki is designed from the ground up to ensure **100% Privacy, Absolute Data Security, and Zero Third-Party Data Sharing**.

---

## 🛡️ Privacy Architecture Highlights

| Privacy Guarantee | How Nikki Protects Your Data | Third-Party Data Sharing? |
| :--- | :--- | :---: |
| **1. 100% Local AI Model Execution** | Nikki runs locally on your PC GPU/CPU or phone via **Ollama / llama-cpp**. Your prompts and text never leave your machine. | ❌ ZERO |
| **2. Zero Third-Party API Keys** | Nikki requires NO paid cloud API keys (No OpenAI, No Anthropic, No Google Cloud, No AWS). | ❌ ZERO |
| **3. Offline Mode Operations** | All core capabilities (voice listening, TTS speaking, file editing, command execution, phone calling, system security, AI teaching) work completely offline without internet! | ❌ ZERO |
| **4. Local Encryption & Storage** | All memories, knowledge bases, custom skills, and user preferences are stored strictly inside your local workspace (`memory/` folder). | ❌ ZERO |
| **5. No Tracking or Analytics** | Nikki contains zero telemetry, zero analytics trackers, zero background logging to cloud servers. | ❌ ZERO |

## 📂 Where Your Data Lives

All of your data is stored locally on your own hard drive inside:
`C:\Users\ABC\.gemini\antigravity\scratch\local_ai_assistant`

- 🧠 `memory/long_term_memory.json` – Your local memory database.
- 📚 `memory/knowledge_base.json` – Learned topics.
- 🧬 `custom_skills/` – Custom programmed skills.
- 💬 Messaging logs & audio processing happen strictly in RAM and local disk.

**Your data belongs 100% to you, stays on your device, and is never shared with any 3rd party!**
